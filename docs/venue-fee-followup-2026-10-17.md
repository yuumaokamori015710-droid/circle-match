## Cancellation

- Four Fuchu gyms and the soccer ground: [city cancellation guide](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/shisetu/supotu/yoyaku/torikesi.html), 100% refund by one calendar month before use, 50% by ten days, none from nine days. Separate outdoor-weather procedures are not applied to indoor gyms.
- Four Komae facilities: their linked operator guides, online cancellation by three days; self-cancellation has no refund or transfer. Outdoor bad-ground/weather and WBGT >=28 non-use exceptions remain explicitly outdoor-only.
- Two Shinagawa gyms: current [January 2026 operator guide](https://www.ssa.or.jp/vc-files/sougou/r080105riyougaidobukku.pdf). Cancellation charge 50% by one month (equipment excluded), 100% within one month (equipment included); refunds at the counter, including prior online payments.
- Square Ebara: [operator use guide](https://www.shinagawa-culture.or.jp/square/facility/sports/usage.html), same percentage bands, payment before cancelled use time, counter refund.
- Two Taito facilities: [official cancellation guide p41](https://www.city.taito.lg.jp/kusei/online/yoyaku/riyosyaguide.files/4.pdf). Riverside gym: full by two months, 80% by fifteen days, 50% by seven, none from six days. Tanaka sports: 80% by fifteen, 50% by seven, none from six; do not apply its meeting-room full-refund rule. Online-only payments can use online cancellation/refund unless more than 185 days after payment; mixed-payment/weather cases have counter procedures.
