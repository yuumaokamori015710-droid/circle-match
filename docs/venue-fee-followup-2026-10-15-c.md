# Venue fee follow-up: 2026-10-15, third batch

Reviewed 20 Tokyo catalog facilities against municipality or facility-operator pages. Ten previously unpriced facilities gained source-matched tariffs; ten remain unpriced because a current charge for the cataloged bookable surface and sport was not established. No venue IDs, original booking URLs, reservations, inquiries, or private data were changed.

All amounts have an official source URL, verification date, booking unit, and applicable conditions in `outputs/venues/catalogs/fee_plans.json`. Where a site publishes different time periods, each remains a separate range. There is no zero or inferred substitute for a missing price. The verified cancellation rules are in `outputs/venues/catalogs/cancellation_policies.json`; the lack of a policy entry means cancellation terms remain unknown. This catalog does not guarantee real-time availability.
