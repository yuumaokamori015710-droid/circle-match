# Official Venue Fee Review: 2026-10-22

## Scope And Results

Reviewed 20 existing unpriced Tokyo records, prioritizing soccer fields and
basketball/volleyball gyms in Nishitokyo, Chofu, Hino, Kunitachi and Akishima.
Added 20 sourced tariff sets and 16 cancellation-condition records. This is
20 facilities, not the number of individual tariff rows or web pages.

The catalog remains 1,206 records: **749 priced and 457 unpriced**, including
7 suspended unpriced records (**450 active unpriced**). Five of this batch's
free facilities have explicit official free-use statements; unknown is not zero.
No venue IDs, booking URLs, source catalogs, prior reviews or user data changed.

## Cancellation And Source Decisions

- Nishitokyo (4): [current guide](https://www.city.nishitokyo.lg.jp/enjoy/sports/sports_shisetu/50981020151125.files/homepage.pdf) confirms seven-day full refunds, the restricted late-booking four-day half-refund exception and facility weather decisions 60 minutes before use. The [2026 system rules](https://www.city.nishitokyo.lg.jp/enjoy/sports/sports_shisetu/20260127101956730.html) confirm in-person cash refunds even for online payment.
- Chofu (4): the three outdoor pages and Omachi's complex page confirm previous-day web cancellation and one-/three-month new-reservation suspensions. Outdoor rain exceptions are not copied to the indoor gym. The separate school-opening guide has a different deadline and is not applied here.
- Hino (4): [Minamidaira's operator guide](https://hino-minamidaira-gym.com/guide) confirms web cancellation through three days before, direct contact from two days before and nonrefund of advance payment. The grounds' common page confirms advance-payment nonrefund only; it does not establish a free-cancellation deadline or weather exception. No such details are invented.
- Kunitachi riverside record (1): both soccer/baseball pages agree on full refund through five days before, and rain refund application within 14 days including the use date. Application at the gym counter, card and receipt remain necessary.
- Akishima (3): the sports center explicitly rejects cancellation after reservation confirmation. Mihori's ordinance establishes nonrefund in principle with an exceptional administrative decision. Ogami's page establishes weather-related unusability, not a general free-cancellation deadline.
- The operator's Fureai website currently redirects to maintenance. The identical revision is still directly published and linked by Hino City; the municipal copy was downloaded and visually checked. Old and revised columns, including the published half-court 5,900-yen rounding, were distinguished.
- Akishima's old `r703sisetuguide.pdf` returned 404 on direct retrieval. Its cached search text was not used as the current price source. The current city HTML, linked fee PDF and Mihori ordinance replaced it. A failed fetch was not retried to evade a restriction.
- Omachi's gym-specific HTML could not be retrieved by the web tool. The city's April 2026 revised guide was directly downloaded and its gym table visually checked. The tennis and meeting-room fees were excluded.
- No new cancellation entry was invented for Chofu's general gym, Fureai Hall, Hino's track or FSX Arena.

## Preservation, Validation And Publication

The append-only importer and regression tests are
`scripts/record_venue_fee_review_20261022.py` and
`scripts/test_venue_fee_followup_20261022.py`. Existing three source catalogs
remain unchanged; earlier fee/policy records are checked against baseline
`c8b28c6`. Import is idempotent and refuses conflicting existing reviews.

Private evidence lives in unpublished `work/venue-fee-review-20261022/`:
official response URLs, timestamps, hashes, saved PDFs, extracted tables,
rendered PDF evidence and local/production verification reports. No personal
credentials or authenticated responses were collected. No forms were sent.

Validation: 113 venue regression tests passed, including the 12 new focused
checks. API/server-rendered HTML for all 20 records matched the reviewed
tariffs, source links, original booking links and cancellation text. Local
mobile/desktop checks passed 48 cases (390px: 20, 768px: 4, 1440px: 20,
1920px: 4), including expanded tariff tables and unclipped price text. Screenshots
were visually checked at 390px and 1440px. Production publication evidence is
recorded in that private evidence directory. The user's production browser has an explicit
desktop display preference; that preference is preserved rather than changed
to claim a production mobile pass.

Publish with latest main, not a historical deployment. Rollback is a normal
revert of this review commit followed by deployment; no production DB migration,
environment-variable change or paid plan is involved.
