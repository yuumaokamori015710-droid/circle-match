# Official Venue Fee Review: 2026-10-23

## Scope And Results

Reviewed 20 existing unpriced Tokyo records, prioritizing soccer/futsal fields
and basketball/volleyball gyms in Higashikurume, Kiyose, Inagi, Musashino and
Kokubunji. Added **18 tariff sets and 13 cancellation-condition records**.
Two records remain unpriced because one field was abolished and the other
center's first gym is suspended. This counts facilities, not tariff rows.

The catalog remains **1,206 records: 767 priced and 439 unpriced**, including
8 suspended unpriced records (**431 active unpriced**). Two free facilities
have explicit official free-use statements and retain registration conditions.
Unknown prices remain unknown, not zero. All checks below are dated 2026-10-23.

## Reviewed Facilities

Resident/nonresident, age, weekday/weekend and time-of-day conditions remain
in tariff labels and notes. Ordinary-use ranges exclude separately charged
lights, air conditioning, equipment and admission/participation surcharges.
Prices do not grant commercial, tournament or unattended-booking permission.

## Cancellation And Source Decisions

- Higashikurume's three current fields: [current ordinance, Article 8](https://www2.city.higashikurume.lg.jp/reiki_int/reiki_honbun/g146RG00000302.html) establishes nonrefund in principle and an administrative special-reason exception. No specific free deadline or weather refund percentage is invented. The April 2026 ordinance no longer lists Seibu; the newsletter independently confirms abolition.
- Wakuwaku's heat-refund page appeared in cached search results, but the direct official URL returned 404. It was not treated as a current cancellation policy. No cancellation entry was added for Wakuwaku or the sports center. The latter's exact city half/quarter prices take precedence over formula-based rounding; an operator statement about nonresident personal-use tickets was not extended to group rentals.
- Kiyose outdoor field: 80% refund through 30 days before, 60% through 20 days, 30% through the previous day; weather cases require contact and a refund procedure. Both indoor facilities instead use the **10-day** deadline for 30% refund. Their individual facility pages support each rule, and [the common booking guide](https://www.city.kiyose.lg.jp/shisetsu/riyouyoyaku/1005423.html) was cross-checked. Indoor rain exemptions are not inferred.
- Hirao: the current May 2026 facility page explicitly makes registered groups free. Nominal 510/340-yen amounts in the [general fee table](https://www.city.inagi.tokyo.jp/kanko/sports/1004109/1004114.html) were not charged to those groups. Unregistered/nonresident fees, separate light/AC fees and youth/indoor-futsal restrictions remain explicit.
- Nagamine: [July 1, 2026 rules pp3-4](https://www.inaginagamine-sportsfield.jp/guide/pdf/rule.pdf) specify nonrefund in principle, counter/phone contact for qualifying weather interruption, one-hour-before on-site judgment and the first-30-minute exception. A lightning pause is not generally refundable. The rules prohibit commercial use and internet/SNS recruitment of unspecified participants with a fixed venue/date; the fivefold admission-charge provision is not permission to ignore these prohibitions.
- Inagi general ground and Wakabadai only: [operator weather procedure](https://inagisoutai.jp/request/) requires Web notification from one hour before use until 23:59 the next day; phone notification is not accepted. This is not Nagamine's process, nor a general voluntary-cancellation deadline. The operator's expired 2025 heat-refund campaign was not extended to 2026. No new cancellation entry was invented for Hirao, Tamagawa or the gym.
- Musashino's three facilities: [April 2026 rental guide p4](https://www.musashino.or.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/001/578/202604kashikiri.pdf) establishes 50% cancellation charge through three days before, 100% from two days before. The same-day waiver applies only to first-come Web bookings cancelled on their booking day; unconfirmed lottery cancellation is separate. [Midori's own facility page](https://www.musashino.or.jp/sports/1001550/1001571.html) additionally supports the weather judgment/contact/half-time rules, which are not applied to the indoor gym. The current fee PDF's hatched track slots are unavailable, not zero-priced. Current closure notices are not permission to book during construction.
- Kokubunji sports center: [current booking/refund guide](https://www.city.kokubunji.tokyo.jp/culture-sports-tourism/sports/1011397/1002609.html) confirms full refund through one month before and half through ten days before, with the permit/receipt and stamp at the counter. Outdoor weather-specific refunds are not copied to the indoor gym. Hikari's partial closure is not recorded as whole-facility suspension.

## Preservation And Verification

The importer `scripts/record_venue_fee_review_20261023.py` is idempotent and
refuses conflicting prior fee/policy reviews. Baseline is main commit
`9bea74907fed9d66b553b76ce803cfbfe312ef8f`. IDs, names, original booking URLs,
old fee/policy entries and all unrelated source data are retained. Only two
source-catalog records change: Seibu's closure/date/source and Hikari's
partial-closure note/date/source, retaining prior notes and source entries.
The legacy municipality field of the Kokubunji gym record is not silently
rewritten as part of a price review.

All **124 venue regression tests passed**, including 11 focused Oct23 tests.
The first sandboxed run's local HTTP requests were denied with WinError 10013;
the authorized rerun passed without weakening tests. Expected catalog-failure
test logs are intentional. API/SSR and Playwright checks passed all 18 priced
records plus both availability decisions at 390, 768, 1440 and 1920px
(80 facility/viewport cases), plus four no-JavaScript cases. Exact tariff
arrays, units, source/booking links, all 13 policies, no horizontal overflow,
unclipped prices and no browser errors were verified. Mobile/desktop
screenshots and the key PDF tables/rules/closure notice were visually checked.

Private evidence is in unpublished `work/venue-fee-review-20261023/`:
public response URLs/timestamps/hashes, original PDFs/HTML, extracted text,
rendered evidence and local/production browser reports. No credentials,
authenticated pages, form submissions, bookings or payments were collected.
