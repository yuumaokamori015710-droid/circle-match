# Venue Fee Review: 2026-10-12

- Scope: 975 previously unpriced venues out of 1,202 existing venues.
- Read 805 distinct primary official URLs and 250 linked/discovered official documents.
- Confirmed prices for 306 additional venues: 533 priced, 669 still unconfirmed.
- No venues, IDs, addresses, sport assignments, booking URLs, or existing prices were removed or replaced.
- `venue-fee-review-2026-10-12.json` records the applied values, official sources, selected tariff rows and billing units.

## Interpretation

- Prices are the published facility rental or ordinary karaoke room rates, not inferred hourly estimates.
- Ranges retain differences in room/area, time, day, membership or resident category. Conditions and additional charges are noted per venue.
- Individual gym admission, parking, equipment, discounts, all-day packages and extension fees are excluded from rental ranges unless explicitly stated.
- Karaoke per-person and per-room fees are distinguished. Solo/additional charges and promotional packages are not treated as base rates.
- Ota and Edogawa revised fee tables use the new fee column effective in 2026. Ambiguous merged PDF rows are not applied.
- Conditional ranges, per-person fees and room rental fees are not included in the court-per-hour comparison filter.

## Remaining Unconfirmed

The remaining 669 entries have not been assigned a verified amount. This does not mean all 669 lack published prices. Reasons include incomplete/ambiguous tables, image-only or unreadable documents, rates requiring inquiry, and school-opening eligibility conditions. Do not replace unknown prices with zero or a third-party estimate.

Reservation availability and final charges remain subject to the facility's official booking process. This audit does not book any facilities.
