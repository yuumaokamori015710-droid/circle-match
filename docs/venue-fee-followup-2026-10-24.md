# Official Venue Fee Review: 2026-10-24

## Scope And Results

Reviewed 20 existing unpriced Tokyo records, prioritizing soccer/futsal fields
and basketball/volleyball gyms in Machida, Akiruno, Musashimurayama, Hamura,
Kodaira, Koganei and Mizuho. Added **18 tariff sets and 17 cancellation-policy
records**. Two tariff holds remain unknown, not zero.

The catalog remains **1,206 records: 785 priced and 421 unpriced**, including
8 suspended unpriced records (**413 active unpriced**). Counts are catalog
records, not a claim of complete municipal coverage or live availability.
All source checks and new fee/policy entries below are dated 2026-10-24.

## Cancellation And Source Decisions

- Machida's eight records: the [currently linked detailed booking guide](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/bunka/riyou/snet/snet01.files/guidobook20251009.pdf), PDF p30 (printed p28), requires user cancellation before the day, or before use if unavoidable on the day. Cancellation from three weeks before use is 1pt; no-show is 2pt; cancellation less than 60 minutes after reservation is excepted. These restrict bookings in the second following month, not a verified monetary fee/refund percentage. Outdoor weather decisions are made by the facility 30 minutes before use, with no user cancellation needed and no penalty. They are not automatically applied to the indoor gym.
- Machida's previously indexed `shousai20250401.pdf` now returns 404. The current [guide landing page](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/bunka/riyou/snet/snet01.html) links the October2025 PDF instead. The facility pages and current operator fees were independently checked. No old COVID exception was extended. Basic fees do not prove unrestricted soccer use for baseball/softball records, commercial permission or a higher-purpose tariff ceiling. The gym's [2028-2030 future closure plan](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/bunka/sport/sport/sport07.html) is not a current closure.
- Akiruno's three priced records: [current ordinance Articles12 and26](https://www.city.akiruno.tokyo.jp/reiki/reiki_int/reiki_honbun/g151RG00000222.html) provide nonrefund in principle, with all/part exceptions for responsibility-free unavailability, facility cancellation, or cancellation at least four days before with a reason accepted by the authority. This is not unconditional full refund. [April2026 revision](https://www.city.akiruno.tokyo.jp/0000019291.html), current city facility pages and operator tables distinguish old applications from new applications; old prices and personal/infant free use were excluded. Conflicting wording about resident evidence is not silently relaxed.
- Musashimurayama's three records: the current [city booking page](https://www.city.musashimurayama.lg.jp/shisetus/reservation/1001847.html) links `r80609siyouryou.pdf`, not the deleted R7 file. PDF pp6-7 confirms gym double rates, actual rounded subdivisions, outdoor hourly rates and an upcoming tennis-only change, which was not applied to grounds. [Operator gym guide](https://musashimurayama-gym.jp/gym/guide) and outdoor slot tables corroborate 2h30m / 2h units. A one-hour ground unit is not permission for every booking to last one hour.
- Musashimurayama cancellation: the [flowchart](https://musashimurayama-gym.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/利用中止・返金手続き手順R1.9.1-1.pdf) distinguishes paid cancellation at the counter before use from unpaid reservations (system cancellation at least three days before, otherwise counter). Self-cancellation by phone is not accepted. Receipt and application are required; a month-late claim is not refundable. Outdoor weather/condition cancellation must be reported from the preceding day through one hour after the start. The [July22,2026 heat notice](https://musashimurayama-gym.jp/news/info/6029) separately permits heat-prevention phone cancellation in that window with full refund; no unnotified no-show refund. The indoor entry does not inherit this outdoor heat rule.
- Hamura: [gym booking/refund rules](https://www.city.hamura.tokyo.jp/prsite/0000002165.html) allow 100% through20days before,75% through10days,50% through5days; none from4days. Documents and a transfer/refund process are required. This indoor rule is not copied to Miyanosita park. The [May23 reopening update](https://www.city.hamura.tokyo.jp/0000019660.html) explicitly reopens hall2 on May26 while earlier sections retain closure history. Hall1 remains excluded; the [cancelled refurbishment plan](https://www.city.hamura.tokyo.jp/0000020405.html) does not mean hall1 reopened. City exact rounded outside fees are retained, not blindly multiplied.
- Kodaira's [operator group guide](https://sports-kodaira.jp/group) allows refund for paid cancellation through14days before, requiring the representative's stamp. No late refund rate is invented. The first room's public-event-only restriction is why the ordinary second-room tariff is selected.
- Mizuho town's [facility page](https://www.town.mizuho.tokyo.jp/kyoikuiinkai/about/007/002/p010900.html) gives no refund for user cancellation, no voluntary time/place change, and same-facility transfer for qualifying weather/unavailability reported within five town-office working days. This is not an indoor rain guarantee. A similarly named facility in Mizuho city was not used.
- Akirudai explicitly says free individual use but paid group use with an inquiry-required amount. Koganei's linked PDF was incomplete; old city publications and third-party rates were not enough to establish the current hire tariff. No inquiry, login, booking or payment was performed. Firecrawl returned insufficient credits once; ordinary public-source retrieval completed the review without a paid upgrade or parameter retries.
