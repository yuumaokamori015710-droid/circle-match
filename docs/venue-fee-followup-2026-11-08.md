# Venue fee follow-up: 2026-11-08

Reviewed 25 existing Tokyo records, starting with the four active unpriced
soccer/futsal, basketball and volleyball entries. Added 20 tariffs and 10
cancellation/weather records. The catalog remains at 1,206 records: 978
priced, 228 unpriced (217 active unpriced). No venue IDs, booking URLs, existing
catalog records, or earlier prices and policies were replaced.

- Koganei: the [operator's group guide](https://koganei-sports.jp/gymnasium/group/)
  identifies the main gym as group hire. The municipal education report has
  the area-by-duration table, and the [2026 city comparison](https://www.city.koganei.lg.jp/kosodatekyoiku/shisetsu/kominkan/siryou/kouunshinkaigiroku/37_kaigiroku/37-02kouunshin.files/shiryou_06.pdf)
  confirms the full-room rate. The [city cancellation page](https://www.city.koganei.lg.jp/kurashi/455/shiryo/sportsyoyakuhenkou.html)
  links the cancellation table and explains the online cutoff.
- Hachioji: the municipal education inventory marks the exact Kawamachi,
  Kunugida, and Takakura baseball spaces free. Current facility guides
  [Kawamachi](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/shisetsu/104/p012073.html),
  [Kunugida](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/shisetsu/104/p012075.html),
  and [Takakura](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/shisetsu/104/p012124.html)
  identify the current use and booking conditions. Shibuya's Oyama youth
  baseball fee is also explicitly free, not an unknown amount.
- The operator image for S&D Kaminakahara distinguishes the baseball
  facility including the track from the track-only rate. Separate lighting
  surcharges are not represented as hire prices. Tsukishima, Onan and
  the two illuminated Tachikawa parks publish inclusive night rates.
  City/ward, age, area, and 1-hour, 2-hour, 2.5-hour, 3-hour and 12-hour
  rates are kept separate.
- Hachioji's three cancellation policies were already present and were not
  replaced. Tachikawa's reservation penalties are not cancellation charges.
  The city also specifies conditions under which public paid recruitment
  is treated as a potential commercial activity.
